# Outergy

**A founder-operated portfolio of software products.** [outergy.com](https://outergy.com)

Outergy Group is the holding company behind a growing line of macOS apps, iOS apps, and SaaS ventures. Each product is built to be reliable, useful, and worth paying for, shipped by an operator who has founded, scaled, and sold software before. This repository hosts the Outergy marketing site; the products themselves live at the links below.

Built by Chris Doyle, who previously founded SiteCapture (2014) and sold it in 2021.

---

## The Portfolio

### [Stash](https://yourstash.ai)
A native macOS menu bar app that combines clipboard management with screenshot annotation. Stash keeps a 30-day searchable history of everything you copy, and captures annotated screenshots with a single global hotkey, so the thing you copied an hour ago is always one search away.
*macOS · Productivity · Clipboard Manager*

### [Remind](https://remind.ing)
A macOS app that delivers full-screen meeting reminders with optional AI-generated attendee briefings. Busy professionals never miss a calendar event and always walk in prepared, because the reminder takes over the screen and the briefing is already written.
*macOS · Productivity · Calendar*

### [Degree Daddy](https://degreedaddy.com)
A cold plunge and sauna tracker for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. It logs every session with temperature, duration, and streaks, and syncs cleanly with Apple Health and Strava so recovery work shows up alongside the rest of your training.
*iOS · Apple Watch · Health & Fitness*

### [Dance Party](https://danceparty.ai)
A widget platform that turns any Apple TV into an office display. Sales leaderboards, meeting schedules, Slack-to-screen messaging, walk-up songs, and AI daily digests all run natively on Apple TV and are managed entirely from a browser.
*tvOS · SaaS · Office Tech*

### [50X](https://50x.ing)
An always-on macOS meeting recorder with AI summaries and VoicePrint speaker identification. A built-in knowledge graph links spoken decisions to the written record across Slack, Notion, and Linear, so what was agreed in a call never gets lost.
*macOS · Productivity · AI*

### [Matrix Desktop](https://matrix.watch)
A macOS app that renders film-accurate Matrix digital rain as a live desktop wallpaper and screensaver. Twelve presets, multi-monitor support, and 60fps Metal rendering keep it smooth even on 5K displays.
*macOS · Screensaver · Metal*

### [SiteCapture](https://www.sitecapture.com)
An AI-powered field operations platform for jobsite documentation, quality control, and project reporting across solar, construction, and property management. Founded in 2014 and acquired in 2021, it remains part of the Outergy story as the venture that started it.
*SaaS · Field Operations · AI*

### [DJ Fun Boy](https://djfunboy.com)
A design-system extraction engine and reference library. Point it at any website or app and it produces complete, replicable design documentation, covering colors, typography, spacing, and component patterns, alongside a browsable gallery of curated systems.
*Web · Design Systems · Developer Tools*

### [Design Daddy](https://designdaddy.ai)
An AI logo creator built as a taste-compounding process rather than a one-shot generator. Start from a curated wall of real app icons, refine over rounds as it learns what you like, and leave with the one, delivered as editable SVG code instead of a flat image.
*Web · AI · Design*

### [Joint Chiefs](https://jointchiefs.ai)
An open-source MCP server for multi-model code review. It runs your code past OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Grok, and local models in parallel, then has them debate across rounds until a moderator writes a single consensus. The result is a higher-confidence review than any one model gives on its own.
*Developer Tools · MCP · Open Source*

### [Pongo](https://pongo.ing)
An iOS app for coordinating real-world mahjong games with friends. Propose a game with a date and time, then cascade-invite until four players say yes, with per-game chat, calendar lock-in, and support for multiple mahjong styles.
*iOS · Social · Games*
